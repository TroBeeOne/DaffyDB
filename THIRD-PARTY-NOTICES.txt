derivative of, replacement for, or emulator of Microsoft SQL Server. It is not
affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Microsoft Corporation. Microsoft,
SQL Server, Azure and Visual Studio are trademarks of the Microsoft Corporation.

TDS began at Sybase in the 1980s and has many independent implementations,
clients and servers alike: FreeTDS, jTDS, Tiberius and Babelfish for PostgreSQL
among them.
